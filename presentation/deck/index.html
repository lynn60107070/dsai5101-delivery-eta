Leakage audit

With after-order times, validation MAE falls 6.25 → 4.53 (R² 0.51 → 0.72). Pickup time alone gives almost all of it (4.55). None of it exists at ordering.

B5Backup

Restaurant or road?

Pickup and road each take a median 13.1 min, both ~2.7 min slower at peaks. Late orders lose more on the road in the 7 big districts (72% of late orders), more at pickup elsewhere.

Best at 16 leaves with default settings; at 511 leaves train 2.45 vs validation 6.63. The tuned model uses 76 leaves, held back by a small learning rate, big leaves and strong L2.

Errors by district: 4.4 (district 18) to 9.0 (district 21).

What the model learned

Average prediction as one input changes: about +24 min from 0.2 to 4.9 km, +6 as orders per rider go 0.5 → 3, +4 across the day, +3 slow vs fast restaurants.

Elastic-Net, in minutes: +6.1 per km · lunch +4.8 · dinner +3.6 · no real prep estimate +2.0–2.5 · twice the usual load only +1.1.

Weekend and drift

Same daily shape (r = 0.92); weekend 1–3.5 min slower noon–18:00. One weekend day to learn from, so no weekend feature.

Mon 17 vs Mon 24 differ by 0.5 min per busy hour: no drift within the week.

B10Backup

The 8-minute cliff

35% of promises sit at the 30-min floor; the median order arrives 8.3 min early. Late arrivals drop from 798 to 336 orders across exactly +8 min, at every promise level.

B17Backup

Features we engineered

Main model: 10 features, 33 inputs

Haversine distance · time of day as sine/cosine · lunch and dinner flags · prep estimate + missing and default flags · district load relative to its usual level + flag · district (one-hot, 23)

Extended: 23 features, 46 inputs

+ riders who finished in the district in 30 min · open orders · orders per rider · restaurant's past delivery and pickup times · recent district delays · restaurant queue · prep-estimate bias

Rule for every feature
Only events that had already happened when the order was placed. Tried and rejected: Manhattan distance (same), log target (worse), weekend flag (one weekend day), LightGBM (same accuracy).
